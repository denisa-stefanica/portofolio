How do casual riders and annual members use Cyclistic bikes differently?

Google Data Analytics Capstone, Case Study 1. Tools: Python, SQL, Tableau. View the code on GitHub

Business task

Cyclistic is a Chicago bike-share company. Its director of marketing, Lily Moreno, believes future growth depends on maximizing annual memberships, which finance has found to be far more profitable than single-ride and day passes.

My task was to understand how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently, so the marketing team can design a strategy to convert casual riders into members. The findings go to Moreno, the marketing analytics team and the executive team, who must approve any recommendation.

Data sources

I used Cyclistic’s public historical trip data for the previous 12 months (e.g. Month Year to Month Year), provided by Motivate International Inc. under its public data license. Each monthly CSV file holds one row per trip, including start and end time, bike type, start and end station, and whether the rider is a member or a casual rider.

  • Credibility: the data comes from the company’s own trip logs, is current and is publicly cited. Add your ROCCC assessment.
  • Privacy: there is no personally identifiable information, so I can’t tell where casual riders live or whether one person bought several passes.
